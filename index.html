Notes

Distance & time
Figures are for the complete listed experience from its stated start to its stated finish. Return and loop walks include the full outing back to the finish. One-way walks are measured from start to end.
Grades
Where NSW National Parks publishes a grade for the exact experience, that is shown as the official grade. Where it does not, the app shows a clearly labelled ChatGPT estimate based on distance, terrain and published route descriptions.
Maps
The home and “Where in the park?” maps show practical trailheads. The detailed trail map is separately audited and labelled as either an exact route match or a broader route reference.
Conditions
Track conditions, closures, tides, swell, creek levels and access can change. Check current NSW National Parks alerts and conditions before setting out.
